The chapter is where Imo State University graduates in Ebonyi State find each other.

- **Meet people who share your university.** Members live and work across the state, in many professions.
- **Come to the chapter's meetings.** The chapter meets on the first Sunday of every month.
- **Give something back.** The chapter takes on work for the university and for the community. See
  [Our work](../our-work/).
- **Help those coming after you.** Members who have settled in the state help newer graduates find their
  feet.

Ready to join? See [How to join](../join/).
