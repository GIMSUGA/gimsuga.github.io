---
title: How to join
view: join
---
If you graduated from Imo State University and live in Ebonyi State, you are welcome in the chapter.
Membership of GIMSUGA comes through a chapter.

1. **Talk to a member you know.** New members are introduced to the chapter by an existing member.
2. **Don't know a member yet?** Message or email the chapter below. An officer will talk with you and put
   you in touch.
3. **The executive takes it from there.** They explain what membership involves and how to pay your dues.
   Dues are paid offline, never through this website.
