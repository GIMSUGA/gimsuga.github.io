**Who we are.** This website belongs to the GIMSUGA Ebonyi State chapter. The chapter is responsible for the
personal information described here.

**What the site shows.** The names and roles of the chapter's officers, shown with their agreement. The
chapter's meetings and their reports, and a record of the chapter's work. Photographs of people appear only
with their consent, and are taken down on request.

**Getting in touch.** If you message or email the chapter from the Join page, your message goes to the
chapter's own contact, looked after by an officer. It is used only to answer you and to put you in touch with
a member. This website never shows payment details and never takes payments.

**No tracking.** The site sets no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages;
GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses to keep its service secure.

**Questions or complaints.** Contact the chapter through the details on the [Join](../join/) page. You also
have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
